<p><i>BlownChart</i> is a fast, free, open-source Android home screen launcher, forked from Lawnchair (itself built on AOSP's Launcher3). No root required.</p><p>It exists because three things had no ready-made answer: a launcher that categorizes apps into folders right in the app drawer, stays fast even with a large number of apps and folders, and can fully lock down its own settings and layout - useful once your app drawer has grown large and you don't want it reorganized by accident.</p><p>Built on Lawnchair's solid Launcher3 foundation, so it keeps everything Lawnchair already does well - Material You theming, icon packs, global search, and deep customization - while adding:</p><ul><li><b>App drawer folders:</b> categorize apps directly in the drawer (manually or automatically), with one level of folder-in-folder nesting on both the drawer and the home screen, and drag support between them.</li><li><b>Settings and layout locking:</b> a PIN/fingerprint lock for the launcher's own settings and any exit into system Settings, plus separate app-drawer and home-screen locks.</li><li><b>Reliable backup and restore:</b> covers the full home screen layout, widgets, and grid size in a single pass.</li><li><b>Performance at scale:</b> loading and folder editing tuned to stay fast with hundreds of installed apps and dozens of folders.</li></ul><p>Free and open source under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3), same as Lawnchair.</p>
